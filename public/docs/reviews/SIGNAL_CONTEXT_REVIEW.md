## Evidence boundaries

Every recognized predicate operand retains its occurrence, position and original
instruction. XIC and XIO remain distinct instruction kinds; numeric comparisons
retain their value operands and literals without invented causal signs. ONS
storage remains a separate stateful boundary. Shared condition nodes are reused
to trace direct source relationships without materializing all contact/output
pairs or transitive paths.

Exact-address dossiers include input-only sources, individual coil writers,
other explicit uses and timer/counter member associations. Multiple writes retain
their own actions and guards. A timer member association does not establish a
delay, current value or stateless dependency on the timer's input guard. Absence
of an exact writer or reader does not prove unused logic, absence of overlapping
whole-word writes, physical connectivity or a process dead end.

Routine, exact content-container and rung provenance remain visible. Alternate
source edits are not selected or combined into an effective target equation.
Calls and SFC evidence remain context, not transitive execution paths. Authored
alarm/equipment/sensor hints stay attached to their source rung; they do not
classify an individual coil or spread to neighboring addresses.

Namespace qualification covers complete ancestry, bound aliases and implicit
module naming dependencies. Unqualified bindings remain gaps. Guards follow
their actual DAG children, so sibling paths and later predicates cannot
contaminate earlier outputs.
