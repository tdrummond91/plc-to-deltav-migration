## Meaning and boundaries

Each output retains qualified XIC and XIO operands whose condition nodes occur
inside its own incoming graph and whose exact expanded address matches its
target. Comparisons, ONS storage, sibling paths and later contacts cannot become
self-contact findings. An alias may identify the same exact address; a different
member/index spelling remains a different address.

These are source-presence observations. A self contact does not establish a
seal-in circuit, polarity of the overall guard, a necessary condition, or final
output state. A supported contact may coexist with a binding gap, unknown
transfer or stored-state boundary. Its output's original quality remains visible.

Timer contact observations require a qualified XIC/XIO at a known direct EN,
TT or DN member, a TIMER declaration, valid scalar/static bounds, and no control
type conflict. The original operand preserves authored spelling; the member
category is normalized. Links open the existing control dossier with its
separate producers, resets and stored representations. The association does
not establish a duration, current value, delay requirement, selected source
edit or execution across routines. Counter contacts and comparisons remain in
ordinary condition evidence; they are outside this timer-contact category.

Coil groups use exact target, routine and content-container identity. Every
qualified OTE, OTL and OTU stays a separate occurrence. A group reports set/reset
presence only when it contains both OTL and OTU. Separate edits or routines are
never combined to manufacture a pair. Additional OTE writers remain visible;
no scan precedence or active version is selected.

Authored alarm/equipment/sensor hints remain rung-wide. They are not promoted
to individual output classifications. Physical mappings, shutdown/warning
classification and alarm priority require further source evidence and review.
