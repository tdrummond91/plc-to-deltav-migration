03 / WHEN YOU ARE READY

Read one item with three questions

SOURCE FACT

What is actually present?

Look for the instruction, address, comment, connection or branch in the captured source.

INTERPRETATION

What might it mean?

Read the proposed purpose or grouping. A plausible “PID” or “alarm” label is a starting point for review.

OPEN QUESTION

What would settle it?

Identify the missing source, wiring, runtime value or engineering decision. “Partial” means there are limits to investigate.

An address match does not establish physical wiring or live behavior. A report category does not select a DeltaV class.

YOUR FIRST RESPONSE

One item is enough to begin

On an item’s detail page, choose Prepare review feedback. Select and copy the template into your separate review notes. Keep the report identity and record ID with your answer.

Choose: Checked · Needs correction · Evidence missing · Not applicable

Then explain: What did I check or see? What should it say, or what is still unknown? What evidence supports that answer?

The report does not save or submit review answers. These learning checkboxes do not mark plant logic as reviewed. Keep plant-specific notes and complete reports in the approved work environment.

Where your DeltaV classes fit later

Source behavior and target class allocation are separate decisions. A single source group can map to several classes, and several source fragments can contribute to one target object.

  • A loop may fit AI_PID_AO, or use AI_PID with further classes before an _AO output.
  • Simple alarms may be built into a class. More complex alarm behavior may need a separate or split implementation.
  • An equation may need a different engineering category or target treatment after review.
  • Multiple or nested timers can exceed a chosen class’s capability. Splitting logic, including the proposed use of a DC for another timer in a state, is a future mapping decision.

These are captured requirements for your team’s class library. The current report does not perform those mappings or certify behavior. Keep source evidence and review decisions linked when splitting or combining target objects.
