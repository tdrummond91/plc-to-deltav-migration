Where your DeltaV classes fit later

Source behavior and target class allocation are separate decisions. A single source group can map to several classes, and several source fragments can contribute to one target object.

  • A loop may fit AI_PID_AO, or use AI_PID with further classes before an _AO output.
  • Simple alarms may be built into a class. More complex alarm behavior may need a separate or split implementation.
  • An equation may need a different engineering category or target treatment after review.
  • Multiple or nested timers can exceed a chosen class’s capability. Splitting logic, including the proposed use of a DC for another timer in a state, is a future mapping decision.

These are captured requirements for your team’s class library. The current report does not perform those mappings or certify behavior. Keep source evidence and review decisions linked when splitting or combining target objects.
